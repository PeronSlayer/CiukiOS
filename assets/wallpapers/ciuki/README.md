# Ciuki wallpaper scenes

`Ciuk1.png`, `Ciuk2.png` and `Ciuk3.png` (1448 × 1086 RGB PNG) are the owner's
adaptive wallpaper scenes from the 0.8 line: the original 1672 × 941 artwork
extended with scenery on all sides (2026-10-08) so that a centred,
aspect-preserving fill keeps the dog, the lettering, the lake and the sunset
on 4:3, 16:10 and 16:9 screens. They are CiukiOS project assets of the owner,
all rights reserved; they are not part of the CC0 dedication of `../tiles` and
not derived from the approved portrait.

The image installs them in `/system/wallpapers` (`scripts/build_image.py`,
`WALLPAPERS`); `Ciuk2.png` with `wallpaper_mode=fill` is the default wallpaper
(`apps/desktop/settings.c`, `docs/desktop.md`). SHA-256 of the files: Ciuk1
`b6da7cf5…`, Ciuk2 `ec1938ba…`, Ciuk3 `062cdf83…` (also recorded in the
generation notes kept beside the originals).
