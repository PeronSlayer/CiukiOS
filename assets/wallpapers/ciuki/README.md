# Ciuki wallpaper scenes

`Ciuk1.png`, `Ciuk2.png` and `Ciuk3.png` (1448 × 1086 RGB PNG) are the owner's
adaptive wallpaper scenes from the 0.8 line: the original 1672 × 941 artwork
extended with scenery on all sides (2026-10-08) so that a centred 4:3 crop at
1024 × 768 keeps the dog, the lettering, the lake and the sunset. They are
CiukiOS project assets of the owner, all rights reserved; they are not part of
the CC0 dedication of `../tiles` and not derived from the approved portrait.

The image installs them in `/system/wallpapers` (`scripts/build_image.py`,
`WALLPAPERS`); `Ciuk2.png` centred is the default wallpaper
(`apps/desktop/settings.c`). SHA-256 of the files: Ciuk1 `b6da7cf5…`, Ciuk2
`ec1938ba…`, Ciuk3 `062cdf83…` (also recorded in the generation notes kept
beside the originals).
