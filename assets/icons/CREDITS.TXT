CiukiOS official system icons
============================

Based on Tango Icon Theme 0.8.90.
Upstream icons are released into the Public Domain.
See TANGO.TXT for the original declaration and AUTHORS.TXT for authors.

Authors:
Ulisse Perusin, Steven Garrity, Lapo Calamandrei, Ryan Collier,
Rodney Dawes, Andreas Nilsson, Tuomas Kuosmanen, Garrett LeSage,
Jakub Steiner.

Source:
https://tango.freedesktop.org/releases/tango-icon-theme-0.8.90.tar.gz

Archive SHA-256:
6e98d8032d57d818acc907ec47e6a718851ff251ae7c29aafb868743eb65c88e

Selected generic icons have been converted for the CiukiOS renderer.
The family does not use extracted Microsoft or Apple icon artwork.
No endorsement by Tango's authors is implied.

CiukiOS is dedicated to Ciuki, the dog in the original boot splash.
Computer, About and other identity icons incorporate deterministic
conversions of the owner's approved Ciuki portrait. That portrait and
the CiukiOS composites are separate project assets, not part of
Tango's Public Domain release. This notice grants no new license
for the portrait. The approved portrait must not be redrawn or replaced.
