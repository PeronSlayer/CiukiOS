## Required

1. **Model.** Separate base class, base priority, dynamic boost with expiry, and inherited (donated) priority; effective priority = max, recomputed on every change with an explicit run-queue reinsert. 32 internal levels: idle 0, ordinary base 7, focused native UI base 9, privileged high 13, kernel device service in the reserved high band (the current `P_DEVICE` maps there). Public POSIX/Win32 priority values map onto this table (document the mapping; no ABI change to the public values).
2. **Boosts.** Foreground input boost +2 for 20 ms after actual input delivery to the focused window's owner thread, capped at 15, refreshed (not stacked) by repeated input; I/O completion boost +1 for one 10 ms quantum to the thread unblocked by a device completion (ATA, USB, audio stream readiness), capped at 15, decaying to base after the quantum; focus loss ends the foreground promotion; spurious wakes and polling earn nothing. Starvation: a normal ready thread that waited 100 ms gets one bounded dispatch at 15, then returns to base. All boosts are visible in the task list (`tasks` app columns if cheap) and in the `[cpu]` records (count of boosts granted/expired per window).
3. **Donation.** `kmutex` owners inherit the highest effective priority of their waiters, transitively through chains of mutexes; withdrawn and recomputed on unlock, cancellation, timeout and owner death; cycle detection (a detected cycle is a protected record and the wait proceeds without donation, never a deadlock by design); no donation across device waits. The FIFO `fs_lock` (f1-79) keeps its ticket order; donation applies to the holder.
4. **Quanta and budgets.** Keep the 10 ms ordinary quantum and the current 2 ms DOS/audio-sensitive policy; device service aggregate budget ≤ 4 ms per 10 ms under the admitted reference load, exhaustion requeues ordinary work, an urgent audio deadline is served and reported; focused DOS preference uses at most 80 % of the CPU left after device service (reconcile with the existing 8/10 policy and say what changed).
5. **Host evidence.** Tests: input/focus changes, I/O completion boost and decay, two-level priority inversion resolved by donation, waiter cancellation and timeout, cycle detection, device saturation with the audio deadline held, a CPU-hog VM with the desktop still dispatched. Metrics under the admitted load: input-to-UI-ready dispatch ≤ 10 ms, normal ready wait ≤ 100 ms, all R1 bounds and audio deadlines hold together; report dispatch/wakeup counts and the scheduler's CPU overhead before/after (host `-O2` cycles per schedule and per tick). `ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=0 bash scripts/test/host_kernel_tests.sh`, `bash tests/host/audio/run.sh`, `python3 scripts/build_kernel.py`; `python3 scripts/gen_api_reference.py` and `make CAP= apps` if a public header changed.
6. **Report** `build/codex/f0-45-report.md`: sources (what was found of the original 98 scheduler documentation and what remains policy), the model, before/after tables, overhead, files, remaining.
