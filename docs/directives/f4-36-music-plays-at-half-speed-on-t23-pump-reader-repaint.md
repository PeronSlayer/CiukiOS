# f4-36 — Music on the T23 plays at less than half speed: 1518 starvations in 70 s with an empty reservoir, the pump keeps the device queue at 5 ms, the view repaints the whole window on every snapshot, the desktop burns 50 % of the CPU

- **Phase:** F4 (media), F2 (desktop load). **Model:** gpt-6.1-sol / xhigh. **Worktree:** `wt/f4-36` from main (after `a06f0dfb`).
- **Authority:** `apps/music/` (`player.c`, `runtime.c`, `view.c`, `main.c`, `decoder.c`), `apps/libdeskui` only for partial invalidation helpers that f2-84/f2-85 already added (`du_invalidate`), `tests/host/desktop/` Music tests and benchmark, `docs/design/music-player.md` and the Music section of `docs/design/f4-audio-media-contract.md`.
- **Not yours:** the kernel audio device, the stream ABI, the scheduler (f0-45 merged: input/I/O boosts exist now), the storage layer.

## Evidence (T23, image 4bcd1d43, `legacy/local/physical/4bcd1d43/serial.log` and the owner's video `legacy/local/physical/4bcd1d43/video.mp4`, read-only)

- Three playbacks of the same 48 kHz MP3 (first from the stick, then twice from `/Documents/Desktop`): `[music] close starvations=128`, `=707`, `=1518`. In the third session (serial lines 2343–3034, about 70 s) there is no storage contention at all (zero `vfs_wait`), the transport is healthy (`[audio] transport running … halts=0 underruns=0 lead_periods=8`), and every starve record reads `[music] starve ms=0–55 reservoir_frames=0`: the ring is empty each time, about 21 times per second.
- The video shows the position advancing 4 s in 9 s of wall time (0:24 at t=3 s, 0:28 at t=12 s) with gaps of 300–600 ms every few seconds in the recorded sound: the player delivers less than half of real time.
- CPU in that session: `[cpu] top=desktop#17:480–508k…` (the shell UI thread at 48–51 %, kernel share 130–320 ‰), `music#39:170–177k0s0` (17 %, no kernel time), `music#38:105–114k` (11 %), `music#37:88–93k` (9 %), `idle_pm=0–2`. The CPU is saturated and the shell is the largest consumer while only the player is open.
- Code (f4-34 state): the pump writes to the device only while `queued<256` and at most `256-queued` frames per call (`player.c:291–294`), i.e. it keeps the kernel stream at 5.3 ms of audio although the stream holds 2048 frames (43 ms); any pump delay above 5 ms is silence at the DAC. The runtime pumps on a 2 ms timed wait (`runtime.c` worker). The reader decodes up to 1024 frames, then `reader_yield()` (`ciuki_raw_yield`), then continues (`player.c:63–89`); it never signals the pump and the pump never signals the reader after consuming (`space` is signalled only by stop/flush). `music_update()` sets `u->dirty=1` (full window repaint) on every snapshot (`view.c:35–67`), and `main.c:85` takes a snapshot and calls it on every UI tick; the `buffering` flag toggles ~20 times per second, so the status label changes and the whole window is repainted at that rate; the shell composites each repaint.
- Hypothesis to confirm on the host (not a conclusion): the reader does not get enough CPU to decode in real time on the T23 (17 % of a saturated CPU behind an interactive-priority shell thread doing repaints the player itself provokes), while the 5 ms device queue turns every scheduling delay into a gap; the "Buffering…" toggling feeds the repaint load back into the shell. The decoder's own cost on a Pentium III is unmeasured; the host figure (30 µs per MP3 frame at -O2) does not transfer.

## Required

1. **Measure first, on the host model that reproduces the T23 shape.** Extend the Music host model with a CPU-contention scenario (a competing thread consuming 50 % with higher priority, scheduling slices of 10 ms) and a decoder cost scale factor (×10, ×30 to stand in for a P3 without SIMD); record reservoir level per second, reader frames/s, pump writes/s, device queued min/max, repaints/s. Reproduce "reservoir 0 at every pump turn" and the half-speed position before changing anything; report which factor is sufficient.
2. **Pump policy.** Keep the kernel stream near full: write whenever `queued < 2048 - 256` up to the free space (the kernel accepts partial writes), on every pump turn; position stays exact through `latency_frames` (f4-31), which already subtracts the device queue. The 256-frame rule remains only for the very first write of a track (start-up latency). The pump turn remains 2 ms while playing; measure its CPU.
3. **Reader throughput and wake protocol.** The reader fills toward a full ring (2.7 s) without yielding after every 1024 frames: yield only when the ring is above 50 %; decode in 4096-frame batches; the pump signals `space` after consuming when free space crossed one batch; the reader runs at normal priority but the pump never waits for it. If the host model shows the decoder itself cannot keep real time at ×30 cost, add the decode-cost record and reduce work (no float output, integer scaler already present; check `MINIMP3_ONLY_MP3`, `MINIMP3_NO_SIMD` and the output conversion path) and state the measured µs per frame before/after.
4. **View and repaints.** `music_update()` repaints only what changed: the status label region, the position label once per second, the progress bar when its pixel position changes; "Buffering…" is shown after 300 ms of continuous starvation and hidden after 2 s without starvation (debounced, no toggling); the snapshot is published to the UI at most 10 times per second and only when a displayed field changed. Target on the host: ≤ 2 window repaints per second while playing without starvation, ≤ 10 during buffering.
5. **Records.** A protected `[music] level` record every 5 s while playing: reservoir min/avg/max frames, reader frames/s, decode µs/frame (measured with the kernel clock on the target), pump writes/s and bytes, device queued min/max, starvations in the window, UI publishes/s and repaints/s. Keep the starve record (bounded to one per second).
6. **Host evidence.** Before/after under the contention scenario: starvations per minute (target 0 at ×10 decoder cost with a 50 % competitor), position advance = wall time ±1 %, repaints/s. `make CAP= apps`, `python3 -B -m unittest discover -s tests/host/desktop -t .`, the Music benchmark; no ABI change expected. Report `build/codex/f4-36-report.md`.
