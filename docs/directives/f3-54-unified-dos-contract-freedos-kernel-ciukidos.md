## Lead decisions (binding unless your research shows one is impossible; then say so with the evidence and propose the nearest alternative)

1. **Identity.** The product stays **CiukiDOS**: boot banner, `VER`, prompt, shell name and the user-visible behaviour the owner knows. FreeDOS is the kernel base, credited as upstream.
2. **Shape.** A fork of the FreeDOS 1.3 kernel (2043) sources, built from the pinned archive by our build with a pinned toolchain, into which the CiukiDOS VMM integrations are ported: the versioned DFVM binding with its ROM gate, FCB and NLS behaviour (f3-50), and the file-call channel to the VMM (f3-49, Windows 9x IFS Manager model, D2). The preferred hook for the file channel is the network redirector interface FreeDOS implements (INT 2Fh AH=11h): confirm from the kernel sources which INT 21h functions reach the redirector, which do not (and need kernel-side changes), and how the SFT/JFT/SDA/CDS ownership of f3-49 maps onto FreeDOS's structures.
3. **CiukiDOS features FreeDOS lacks** are ported, not dropped: inventory them from `floppy_stage1.asm` (INT 21h functions implemented, shell builtins of `SHELL.COM`, the desktop/window integration calls, the `CIUKI_TEST` records, the idle detection of f3-17, the launch protocol of f3-51/f3-51c) and classify each: upstream-equivalent, port into the fork, port into a CiukiOS-specific driver/TSR loaded by `CONFIG.SYS`, or retire with a reason.
4. **Shell.** Decide between FreeCOM 0.85a with a CiukiDOS identity and builtin set, and the current `SHELL.COM` on the new kernel; the decision criterion is compatibility of the games library's batch files and installers plus the owner's known behaviour, with the evidence.
5. **Transition.** The current CiukiDOS kernel stays selectable as a guest until the unified kernel passes the f3-48 conformance battery at 100 % and boots every title of the DOS library; then it is retired by a diary entry. The battery itself does not change for this transition; new cases are added where the unified kernel exposes new behaviour.
6. **Licences.** The unified kernel is GPL-2.0-or-later (FreeDOS kernel); the inventory lists every file's origin and licence, how the source is offered with the image (`system/licenses/` and a source archive or URL per our existing practice in `assets/icons/README.md` and `docs/`), and confirms no incompatible mixing with the CiukiOS kernel (separate binary, separate address space, communication only through the documented VMM ABI).
7. **Toolchain.** Research what builds kernel 2043 faithfully (Open Watcom 1.9/2.0, gcc-ia16, Turbo C variants); choose one that runs on our Linux host, is pinnable by SHA-256 and reproducible; say its licence and the exact archive the lead must pin. `JWasm`, `JWlink`, `djgpp`, `nasm 3.02` are already in `build/external`/the SDK; Open Watcom is not.
