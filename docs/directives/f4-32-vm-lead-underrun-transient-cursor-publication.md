# f4-32 — VM-attached transport: start-up underrun with the four-period lead when the cursor read is transitional; publication must not depend on a coherent progress read

- **Phase:** F4 (audio, all machines). **Model:** gpt-6.1-sol / xhigh. **Worktree:** `wt/f4-32` from main (after `974b509b`).
- **Authority:** `src/kernel/audio/audio_dev.c`, `src/kernel/audio/backend.c`, `src/kernel/drivers/ac97.c`, `src/kernel/include/ciuki/audio.h`, `src/kernel/include/ciuki/audio_vm.h`, `src/kernel/audio/vm_source.c` (re-anchoring only), `src/kernel/probes/f4_probes_vm_audio.c` (lead constants and attribution only), `tests/host/audio/`, `tests/suites/f4-audio.json` (lead numbers only), `docs/validation/f4-31-audio-audit.md` (new section F4-32), `docs/design/` audio contract text if the lead policy wording changes.
- **Not yours:** `scripts/test/run.py`, suite predicates (`deadlines eq 0`, `fresh == blocks`, `frames == 12288` stay), PIC programming, any other driver.

## Why

f4-31 (merged `3ca75c23`) introduced `AUDIO_VM_LEAD 4` (21.3 ms at 48 kHz) for a transport with a DOS VM attached, instead of the former `AUDIO_LEAD 8` (42.7 ms). On the first canonical image with f4-31 (QEMU `1fa1e62e`, main `2277ee3a`) the only VM-audio QEMU case changed its failure mode: before, it failed its `deadlines eq 0` predicate with every PCM check green; now the transport underruns and recovers 21 ms after it starts and the pcm8 check loses one block. The owner's T23 symptom class (DOOM sound lag, music lag) is the same path with a 113 ms worker gap measured on the hardware. The fix must hold for every backend and machine, not for QEMU alone.

## Mechanism to confirm on the host model (hypothesis, not a conclusion)

`audio_service` returns at once when `audio_backend_progress()` reports `-EAGAIN` (`audio_dev.c:263`, "transient coherent-cursor retry, no source consumed") and publishes nothing in that service. `ac97_progress` (`ac97.c:372–393`) treats an observation with `!(status & DCH) && (remaining == 0 || fetched < generation_fetched)` as transitional, retries eight times and returns `-EAGAIN` for up to 2 ms since the first retry (then `deadline_misses++` and `-EIO`). The QEMU AC'97 model (`hw/audio/ac97.c`) moves PCM in bursts at the audiodev timer period (10 ms by default) and raises the buffer-completion IRQ when PICB of the finished buffer reaches 0; a service triggered by that IRQ can observe `remaining == 0` and retry. If every IRQ-time service in the first 21 ms returned `-EAGAIN`, the next wake was the next period IRQ (or the 4 ms deadline), publication never happened, the four published periods drained and the engine halted at `CIV == LVI`; with eight periods the same pattern left four periods of margin and only the 2 ms window occasionally expired (`deadlines=1`). Verify this against the model before changing policy; if the model shows a different cause, report it and fix that.

## Required

1. **Host replay of the QEMU observation pattern.** Extend the host AC'97/DMA model in `tests/host/audio/` with a burst cursor mode: CIV advances in 10 ms steps, PICB reads 0 for the finished buffer at the IRQ instant and reloads at the next step, IRQ at each completion. Reproduce `start published=4` → `underrun published=4 retired=3` with zero publications on the current code, and record the replay (before/after table) in the report.
2. **Publication must not depend on a coherent progress read.** A transitional cursor (`-EAGAIN` from progress) must not end the service without publishing when slots are provably free from the last committed `published/retired`: `ac97_publish` refuses only `slot == civ || slot == piv` from its own observation, so filling the lead can never overrun the engine. Keep the Linux intel8x0 rule already in the code: never commit a transitional fetched/retired position, never fabricate retirement. Apply the same rule to `backend.c` (`audio_dma_*`) for ES137x/SB16/VIA.
3. **Worker wake after `-EAGAIN`.** Re-arm the refill worker within 1 ms (not the next period IRQ, not the 4 ms deadline) until the observation is coherent or the 2 ms retry window becomes a deadline miss. No busy polling: a `P_DEVICE` thread blocks between services (f1-18 rule, `audio_worker_wait` comment).
4. **VM lead by evidence.** Decide `AUDIO_VM_LEAD` from numbers, not taste: it must survive one missed service plus one period at the observed IRQ cadence (QEMU bursts of 10 ms; ICH3 period IRQ every 5.33 ms) with one period of margin, and the f4-31 rule stays: a measured gap grows the session lead and is exposed in status/records. Write the arithmetic in the `audio.h` comment and in the audit amendment. The ≤ 30 ms VM latency target of f4-31 holds only while no gap has been measured. `AUDIO_VM_LATENCY`, the probe's `case=transport` fields and the `latency_frames`/lead expectations of the vm-audio cases in `tests/suites/f4-audio.json` follow the decided value; nothing else in the suites changes.
5. **Recovery without source loss.** The recover-stop/recover-start lost 127 output frames of VM PCM (one 64-unit block at 8 kHz). The retained quantum and the VM source timeline must re-anchor so that a recovery inserts silence instead of dropping source frames (`fresh == blocks`, `frames == 12288`, `late` unchanged). If a period must be lost for a reason you can prove from the ICH3 ring semantics, state the proof and the audible consequence (one click per recovery) in the report; prefer no loss.
6. **Deadline misses.** The pre-f4-31 red (`deadlines=1`) came from the 2 ms cursor-retry window expiring during a burst. With items 1–3 the case must report `deadlines=0` without widening the T23 deadline semantics; document the retry-window decision with the ICH3 datasheet (13.2, BM status/PICB/CIV/LVI) and Linux `intel8x0.c` references.
7. **Stretch attribution.** `stretch_max_ms=76 task=probes resched_lat_max_ms=66` means a `P_DEVICE` wake waited 66 ms behind the probes task. Find the probe or kernel section responsible (candidates on the probe path: `dosvm_create`, `play_boot`, `play_type`, the `dosvm_text` scans, `audio_vm_stats`/`dosvm_audio_info` copies under `irq_save`, `block_check`) and bound it (`kpreempt_point`, copies outside IRQ-off sections), or prove it cannot occur on the T23. Any section longer than 10 ms with preemption or IRQs disabled on the audio or VM paths is a defect; fix it within your authority or name the file and line for the lead.
8. **Host coverage and report.** `bash tests/host/audio/run.sh` with the new burst, re-arm, lead and no-loss cases (failing before the fix where applicable, passing after), `ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=0 bash scripts/test/host_kernel_tests.sh`, `python3 scripts/build_kernel.py`; after a public header or ABI change `python3 scripts/gen_api_reference.py` and `make CAP= apps`. Report `build/codex/f4-32-report.md`: the confirmed or refuted mechanism, before/after replay tables, the lead arithmetic, the stretch attribution, files changed, remaining. Amend `docs/validation/f4-31-audio-audit.md` with a section "F4-32".
