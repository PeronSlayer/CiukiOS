## fx-17 USB stick: read-only mount, move instead of copy across drives, I/O error and unmount at the end

Evidence: `[usb-storage] ready … mode=rw_capable` then `[usb-storage] volume drive=E part=1 fat=32 mode=ro`; `[files] job=move start items=1 bytes=7139466` (a drag from E: to C:); `[files] job=move done … errors=1`; `[storage] readonly reason=write_error file=CIUKI.CFG`; the owner saw an I/O error near the end and the stick disappeared. Required (Windows 98 Explorer/VFAT behaviour): removable FAT volumes mount read-write when the medium allows it (write-protect honoured); a drag between different drives copies, within a drive moves, Shift/Ctrl/right-drag choose; a move whose source cannot be deleted keeps both copies and says so, never reports a failed copy; a write error reports the file and leaves the volume mounted unless the device is really gone. Cover copy/move/delete/rename to and from USB, floppy and C:, including large files and removal mid-copy. Opus, high.
