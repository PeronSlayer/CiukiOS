# f4-35 — VM audio source: late Sound Blaster frames are dropped instead of delayed, so DOOM sound effects get cut

- **Phase:** F4/F3 (VM audio). **Model:** gpt-6.1-sol / xhigh. **Worktree:** `wt/f4-35` from main (after `3357582f`).
- **Authority:** `src/kernel/audio/vm_source.c`, `src/kernel/audio/mixer.c` only if the VM mix needs a hook, `src/kernel/vm/dosvm_sb16.c` and `dosvm_dma.c` (block production and timestamps only), `src/kernel/include/ciuki/audio_vm.h` (statistics), `src/kernel/probes/f4_probes_vm_audio.c` (new case), `tests/host/audio/` and `tests/host/dosvm_audio_test.inc`, `tests/suites/f4-audio.json` (new predicates for the new case only), `docs/design/f4-audio-media-contract.md`, `docs/validation/f4-31-audio-audit.md` (section F4-35).
- **Not yours:** the transport lead policy (f4-31/f4-32), DPMI dispatch, the native stream path.

## Evidence (T23, image 82fadce3, `legacy/local/physical/82fadce3/serial.log`, read-only)

- The owner: "DOOM funziona, va sempre migliorata la gestione audio che a volte taglia gli sfx".
- During DOOM (VM 1, SB16 8-bit 11025 Hz auto-init DMA, `cmd=c6`): the transport is healthy (`[audio] transport running … halts=0 underruns=0 lead_periods=8`), the cadence is healthy (`[vm-audio] vm=1 cadence blocks=431 late_acks=0 merged=0 ack_max_ms=4–7 irq_latency_avg_us=901–1142 underruns=0`), but the source counter `late` keeps growing: `[vm-audio] vm=1 native_refill us=2472 gap_us=16117 sb_lag_frames=48 irq_service_frames=38812 late=70` (48 at the start of the session, 70 after a few minutes on 82fadce3; 407→616 within one minute on the 9af46936 trial). `late` is `stats.late_frames`: VM source frames whose timeline position had already passed when the mixer fetched them, dropped at `vm_source.c:415–427` ("Drop the late frames b.offset .. p-1", `AUDIO_VM_BLOCK_DROPPED`).
- A dropped run of frames at the start of a DMA block is exactly a cut at the attack of a sound effect: DOOM's mixer (DMX) writes each new effect into the next block; when the block's frames are already "late" they are discarded instead of played a few milliseconds later.
- The timeline rule (`AUDIO_VM_FLOOR`, re-anchoring in f4-32, `lag`/`lead` statistics) decides lateness from the VM clock versus the output clock. Which side drifts is not established: the SB16 model's block timestamps, the VM clock (PIT 1 kHz ticks under the monitor), or the output clock after the f4-32 lead change.

## Required

1. **Measure before changing policy.** Add to the vm-audio cadence record the distribution of lateness: how many frames were late by < 1 ms, 1–5 ms, 5–20 ms, > 20 ms, and the lag statistics (`lag`, `lag_min`, `lead`, `lead_min`, `lead_max`) at the time of the drop; reproduce on the host with the production SB16 block producer and a 11025 Hz auto-init stream under a jittered service schedule (f3-42/f3-44 host replay fixtures) and report the histogram.
2. **Late is delayed, not dropped.** Frames that arrive late by less than a bound (decide it from item 1 and from the DOOM/DMX block size: for example up to one DMA block, 64–512 units, or 50 ms, whichever is smaller) are played in order, shifting the source timeline forward by the lateness (re-anchor, counted in `reanchors`), so that no effect loses its attack; only frames later than the bound are dropped, and the drop is recorded with its size. The source lead must stay bounded (it cannot grow without limit: the bound and the shift are the budget), and OPL events keep their relative timing.
3. **Clock source.** Establish which clock drifts: compare the SB DMA block cadence (edges per second from `vm_beat_sb`) with the VM PIT and with the output rate on the host model and on the T23 records; if the VM clock runs slow under load (descheduled ticks), the source anchoring must follow the DMA block cadence rather than the VM tick. Document the decision with the SB16 programming reference (Creative SB16 Hardware Programming Guide, auto-init DMA and the 8237 terminal count) and the DOOM DMX behaviour you can cite.
4. **Regressions.** Host cases: late-by-5-ms block played intact; late-by-100-ms block dropped and recorded; DOOM-like 64-unit blocks at 11025 Hz under 20 ms service jitter: zero dropped frames, source lead within budget; the existing vm-audio QEMU cases keep `fresh == blocks`, `frames == 12288`, `late` unchanged for their healthy schedule. A new QEMU case `vm-audio-jitter-qemu-t23` (written, unrun) exercises the jittered schedule through the probe.
5. **Verification.** `bash tests/host/audio/run.sh`, `ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=0 bash scripts/test/host_kernel_tests.sh`, `python3 scripts/build_kernel.py`; no ABI change expected (statistics live in the private VM stats struct; if `audio_vm_stats` is public, regenerate the API reference). Report `build/codex/f4-35-report.md` with the histogram, the clock analysis, before/after and remaining.
