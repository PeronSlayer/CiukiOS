## Lead amendment 1 (2026-10-25 20:40): decisions on your report

1. **Pixel formats:** you may edit `sdk/win32/gdi32/pixelformat.c` (and its tests) for this: expose real double-buffered RGBA descriptors 16-bit (565) and 32-bit (XRGB, 24 meaningful RGB bits as on Windows: cColorBits 32 with 8/8/8 shifts, cAlphaBits 0 or 8 as you document), with D16 and D24 depth and S8 stencil variants, keeping the existing indices stable; implement D24/S8 in opengl32 (depth precision and stencil test/ops/masks per GL 1.1) — Quake III's default asks for 32-bit color, 24-bit depth, 8-bit stencil and falls back.
2. **WGL_EXT_swap_control:** accept its absence for this milestone (Quake III ignores r_swapInterval without it); document.
3. **PFD_GENERIC_FORMAT:** CiukiOS's opengl32 is the system OpenGL driver (the equivalent of a vendor ICD, whatever its backend), not Microsoft's generic GDI implementation; its pixel formats therefore do not carry PFD_GENERIC_FORMAT, so Quake III runs without `r_allowSoftwareGL`. Document the decision with the PIXELFORMATDESCRIPTOR reference.
4. Continue the speed work on the Quake III hot paths if your profile still shows headroom (the PIII model gives ~6 fps at 320x240: report which costs dominate now). Leave changes uncommitted; the lead reruns LeakSanitizer builds.
