Adapted portions: register constants, voice/rate/control packing, codec
transactions and revision quirks, PCM capture/playback prepare, start/stop
and cursor algorithms. No ALSA driver framework is compiled into CiukiOS.
Modification: replaced Linux/ALSA services with CiukiOS resource claims,
bounded elapsed deadlines, constrained pinned rings, copied capture with
generation tokens, IRQ ownership and permanent failed-stop quarantine.
Targets: src/kernel/drivers/trident.c, src/kernel/drivers/ali5451.c,
src/kernel/audio/voice.c. No firmware or vendor driver binaries are imported.
