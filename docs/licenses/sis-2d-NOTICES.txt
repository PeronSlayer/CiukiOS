Core engine and PCI generation mapping:
Linux v6.12, drivers/video/fbdev/sis/{sis_accel.c,sis_accel.h,
sis_main.c,sis_main.h,sis.h}.
https://github.com/torvalds/linux/tree/v6.12/drivers/video/fbdev/sis

The core is a native adaptation of register encodings and engine fragments,
not an imported Linux fbdev subsystem. Author and origin headers are retained.

X.Org xf86-video-sis, commit 0f50f8c3db2b9f1c9d4ecab8ad278e9db6418a92,
