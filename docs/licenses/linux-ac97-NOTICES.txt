Linux AC'97 driver adaptations in CiukiOS (f1-107)
================================================

Origin: https://github.com/torvalds/linux/tree/v6.12
Selected licence: GNU General Public License version 2 (project LICENSE).
All original GPL-2.0-or-later / GPL-2.0+ grants below remain available for
the upstream portions. No complete ALSA driver or Linux framework is imported.
Ciuki modifications adapt IDs, masks and register algorithms to its existing
leases, bounded deadlines, DMA, mixer and Device Manager interfaces.
