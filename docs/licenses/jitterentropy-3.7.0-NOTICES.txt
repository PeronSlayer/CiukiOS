Kernel import: base, GCD, health, noise and SHA3 implementation, compiled at -O0.
Source is extracted only into build/external; no libc or internal timer thread.
Ciuki's platform header replaces the OS-specific header through a forced include.
The recorded patch adds finite deadline/round checks at measurement boundaries
and an optional buffered raw-delta observer before health testing/filtering.
It does not change conditioning, health cutoffs, OSR or the randomized workloads.
The SHA3 unit in this pin includes SHAKE-256/XDRBG conditioning.
Ciuki files outside the upstream tree carry their own license identifiers.
No target entropy qualification or NIST validation is claimed.
