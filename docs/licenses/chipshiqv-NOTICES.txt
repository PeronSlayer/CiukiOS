X.Org xf86-video-chips native HiQV adaptation
Origin: https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/xorg/driver/xf86-video-chips
Readable upstream-history mirror: https://github.com/freedesktop-unofficial-mirror/xorg__driver__xf86-video-chips
Revision: 9187d76bd4f8d500fc3403b6d4a7bef55508bf2b (commit pin; no release tag substituted)

ct_accel.c: solid fill, directional copy and mono pattern sequences.
ct_cursor.c: cursor base, position, enable and extended palette protocol.
ct_driver.c: PCI IDs, register offsets, MMIO and panel/scratch constraints.
ct_BltHiQV.h: register constants and XR20 polling erratum, no original
licence notice present. No licence is invented for that header; owner
decision in f2-122 allows this source with provenance preserved.
ct_BlitMM.h was researched only; its non-HiQV register layout is not used.
No full X server/DDX or DEC host-upload helper is imported.
CiukiOS glue and safety checks: GPL-2.0-only.
