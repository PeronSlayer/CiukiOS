Ciuki adapts the XY FILL/COPY packet fields, format/ROP constants and ordered
ring publication into src/kernel/drivers/video/intel2d/engine.c. It does not
import Haiku's GART allocator, PCI activation, timeout continuation or debugger.
The register reference in driver.c is independently expressed; its origin is
the above private header and Intel's original chipset documentation.

The gen3_emit_breadcrumb / __gen2_emit_breadcrumb sequence (also selected by
Linux for gen2) and gen2_emit_flush are adapted in engine.c, retaining MI_FLUSH,
16 scratch and 8 sequence stores, plus gen2's twelve scratch-store/flush pairs.
Polling replaces the final user interrupt. Linux intel_engine_cs.c, intel_engine_regs.h,
intel_engine.h, intel_gpu_commands.h, intel_ring_submission.c and intel-gtt.c
are primary reference sources; their complete implementations are not copied.
The bounded HIC-flush protocol is independently expressed from intel-gtt.c's
i830_chipset_flush, with error propagation, an absolute deadline and yielding.
Intel GPU Tools tag intel-gpu-tools-1.5, lib/instdone.c, lib/intel_reg.h and
lib/intel_chipset.h supply the gen2 bit and device definitions, not an imported
implementation. lib/instdone.c credits Intel Corporation (2007, 2009) and
Eric Anholt; lib/intel_reg.h credits Precision Insight, Inc. (1998-1999) and
Keith Whitwell. Their MIT-style reference notices remain with the research
copies under build/research/f2-107/amendment1/. See the reservation design
record for exact paths, lines, links and the per-chip evidence gate.
