Linux v6.12 Cirrus framebuffer engine fragments

Origin: https://github.com/torvalds/linux/tree/v6.12
drivers/video/fbdev/cirrusfb.c -> src/kernel/drivers/video/cirrus/engine.c
include/video/cirrus.h -> src/kernel/drivers/video/cirrus/registers.h

The engine ports cirrusfb_set_blitter, cirrusfb_BitBLT and cirrusfb_RectFill.
CiukiOS replaces Linux glue and unbounded polling with its synchronous
display adapter, bounded completion proof and same-boot quarantine.
Register definitions and original notices are retained in the source files.
